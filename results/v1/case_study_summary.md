## Top 3 disclosure gaps per company (final round)

**Amazon**

1. Governance: GRI 2-11: % women and % underrepresented ethnicities on Board for 2024 and 2025
2. Social: GRI 403-2 absolute RIR and LTIR values for 2019, 2024, and 2025, normalized per 100,000 hours worked
3. Environmental: GRI 302-2 (location-based vs market-based Scope 2 emissions)

**Samsung**

1. Environmental: Location-based Scope 2 emissions (GRI 302-4)
2. Social: GRI 405-2 global gender pay ratio
3. Governance: GRI 2-18: Board performance evaluation methodology, frequency, and outcomes

**Unilever**

1. Governance: GRI 2-11 Board sustainability expertise (number/percentage of directors with credentials)
2. Social: GRI 403-2 (workers exposed to hazardous substances)
3. Environmental: wastewater discharge volume (m³)

**Shell**

1. Governance: GRI 206 political contributions and lobbying expenditures
2. Social: Total working hours for 2024 and 2025 (prevents verification of all frequency rates)
3. Environmental: Water discharge volumes and pollutant concentrations by medium (GRI 303-3, GRI 303-4)

**HSBC**

1. Environmental: GRI 303-1 (absolute water withdrawal in m³)
2. Governance: GRI 205-3: external assurance status of anti-corruption controls
3. Social: TCFD Strategy: social impacts of climate transition (e.g., workforce transitions, community vulnerability, just transition planning)
