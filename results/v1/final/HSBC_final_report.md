### GRI 302 Energy  
In 2025, HSBC reduced its total energy consumption by 34.5% compared with the 2019 baseline [260225-annual-report-and-accounts-2025 (1)_chunk_132]. This reduction was driven by strategic divestments and the adoption of energy conservation programmes, supported by more detailed and automated metering and monitoring [260225-annual-report-and-accounts-2025 (1)_chunk_132]. In 2025, 94.2% of HSBC’s electricity was sourced from renewable sources — a key milestone toward its ambition to purchase 100% renewable electricity across its own operations by 2030 [260225-annual-report-and-accounts-2025 (1)_chunk_132][260225-annual-report-and-accounts-2025 (1)_chunk_135]. The Group follows RE100 principles, prioritising power purchase agreements (PPAs) where feasible; in markets where regulation or energy profiles preclude PPAs (e.g., France and regions of India), it procures renewable electricity through utility partners or energy attribute certificates [260225-annual-report-and-accounts-2025 (1)_chunk_132]. No quantitative data on absolute energy consumption (kWh), intensity (kWh/FTE), or breakdown by fuel type (e.g., grid electricity, natural gas, diesel) is provided beyond the aggregate percentage reduction and renewable electricity share [260225-annual-report-and-accounts-2025 (1)_chunk_132].
