## Top 3 disclosure gaps per company (final round)

**Amazon**

1. Governance: GRI 2-13 board composition data
2. Social: GRI 403-3: % of employees covered by occupational health services and frequency of mandatory health assessments
3. Environmental: GRI 305: Full Scope 3 category-level emissions data for 2025 (all 15 categories)

**Samsung**

1. Governance: GRI 2-18 (Board performance evaluation)
2. Environmental: Location-based Scope 2 emissions breakdown by grid region (GRI 302-2)
3. Social: Global gender pay ratio (basic salary and remuneration of women to men)

**Unilever**

1. Social: GRI 403-3: Occupational health and safety training participation rate
2. Governance: GRI 205-2: total number of corruption-related allegations received, investigated, substantiated, or pending in 2025
3. Environmental: Water withdrawal by source (required by GRI 303-1 and GRI 303-2)

**Shell**

1. Governance: GRI 205-3: political contributions and lobbying expenditures (value and recipients) for 2025
2. Social: GRI 403-10: Work-related illness by diagnosis
3. Environmental: GRI 303-3: Water discharge quality data (e.g., pollutant concentrations)

**HSBC**

1. Governance: GRI 205-1: third-party anti-corruption audit scope/findings
2. Environmental: GRI 303: total water withdrawal (m³) and consumption (m³) for 2025 and 2019, with intensity metrics
3. Social: TCFD Strategy disclosure of social impacts of climate transition (required per rubric)
