### Energy Performance

### Occupational Health and Safety  
Samsung Electronics reported a Lost-Time Injury Rate (LTIR) of 0.022% in 2024, down from 0.023% in 2023 and 0.033% in 2022, based on workplace accidents resulting in one or more days of absence (excluding daily life incidents) [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_146]. The supplier LTIR was 0.035% in 2024, a decrease from 0.064% in 2023 and 0.046% in 2022, measured for Korean suppliers stationed at Samsung sites [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_146]. No major incidents were recorded in 2022–2024 [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_146]. ### Board Composition and Oversight Structure  
As of the end of March 2025, Samsung Electronics’ Board of Directors comprises 3 internal directors and 6 independent directors, with the roles of Board Chair and CEO formally separated to strengthen independence and transparency; independent director Je-Yoon Shin serves as Chair [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_5]. The Board functions as the company’s highest decision-making body and oversees sustainable management activities across environment, society, and governance (ESG) domains [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_5]. To institutionalize this oversight, the Governance Committee was reorganized into the Sustainability Committee under the Board in July 2021; all six independent directors participate in this committee, which deliberates and approves ESG-related management policies, mid- to long-term strategies, and risk management frameworks—including those concerning shareholder return policies [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_5]. The Board’s collective expertise spans finance, law, IT (robotics, AI, semiconductors), risk management, public sector, and sustainable management, as documented in the Board Skills Matrix (BSM); two of the six independent directors are women, reflecting deliberate attention to gender diversity for balanced decision-making [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_5]. The Sustainability Committee reviewed and resolved the “New Environmental Strategy” in September 2022—encompassing long-term climate response and resource circularity goals—and has incorporated key achievements into its agenda annually since 2023 [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_177][Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_11][Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_40].

### Anti-Corruption Governance and Compliance Systems

Whistleblowing mechanisms are operationalized via dedicated channels: 1,238 compliance-related and 930 ethics-related cases were reported in 2024 [Samsung_Electronics_Sustainability_Report_2025_ENG_chunk_137].
