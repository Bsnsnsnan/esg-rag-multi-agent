### GRI 302 Energy

Of this, 189 million MWh was consumed from facilities under Shell’s operational control, with 187 million MWh (99%) sourced from fossil fuels — comprising 40 million MWh from crude oil and petroleum products, 108 million MWh from natural gas, 10 million MWh from other fossil sources, and 28 million MWh from purchased or acquired electricity, heat, steam and cooling from fossil sources [shell-integrated-annual-and-sustainability-report_chunk_829].
