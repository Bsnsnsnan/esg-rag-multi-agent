### GRI 302 Energy  
Amazon reported 131,000 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of carbon-free energy production annually in 2025 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_22]. Carbon-free energy includes electricity from wind, solar, nuclear, hydroelectric, and geothermal sources, as well as site energy contracts and green tariffs that add new renewable projects to the grid [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_14]. AWS aims to procure renewable electricity in the same grids where it consumes electricity; when not feasible, it may procure renewable energy attributes in other locations [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_113]. In 2025, Amazon’s emissions from purchased electricity (Scope 2) totaled 3.74 million metric tons of CO₂e (MMT CO₂e), representing a 34% increase from 2024 and 5% of its total carbon footprint [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_19][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_112]. This growth was driven by electricity use in data centers, electrification of the delivery network, and building electrification [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_19]. Amazon’s global data center portfolio achieved a Water Use Effectiveness (WUE) of 0.12 liters per kilowatt-hour (L/kWh) in 2025—a 20% improvement from 2024 and 52% since 2021—reflecting enhanced energy-water efficiency linkages [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_33][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_35].

### GRI 303 Water  
Amazon achieved 75% progress toward its goal of becoming water positive—returning more water to communities than it uses—across global direct data center operations by 2030 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_33]. In 2025, Amazon’s global leased, owned, and shared data centers withdrew 9.4 billion liters of water [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_33]. Of this, 48% occurred in low water stress regions and 22% in high or extremely high water stress regions, per WRI’s Aqueduct Water Risk Atlas [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_34]. Amazon measured progress using its Water Positive Methodology, which calculates the ratio of reused water plus replenishment volume to total water withdrawal minus water from sustainable sources, multiplied by 100% [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_16]. By end-2025, Amazon had invested in 45 water replenishment projects globally, returning 9.4 billion liters of water to communities and contracting for over 18 billion liters of annual replenishment volume in future years [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_33][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_37]. In India—where Amazon achieved 120% water positivity in 2025, two years ahead of its 2027 target—on-site sewage treatment plants at 28 buildings provided an estimated 298 million liters of recycled water annually, and rainwater harvesting pits collected ~178 million liters for groundwater recharge [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_37].

### GRI 305 Emissions  
Amazon’s total carbon footprint (Scopes 1, 2, and 3) was 80.85 MMT CO₂e in 2025, up 16% from 69.55 MMT CO₂e in 2024 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_113][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_5]. Scope 1 emissions were 15.37 MMT CO₂e, Scope 2 emissions were 3.74 MMT CO₂e, and Scope 3 emissions were 61.74 MMT CO₂e [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_112]. Environmental Attribute Certificates (EACs) delivered a net reduction of 199.4 thousand metric tons of CO₂e (K MTCO₂e) across Scopes 1, 3.1, 3.3, and 3.4—including 41.2 K MTCO₂e reduction in Scope 1 via Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) and Renewable Diesel certificates [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_15][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_113]. Carbon intensity—measured as grams of CO₂e per dollar of revenue—was 112.8 gCO₂e/$Revenue in 2025 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_112][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_5]. Amazon’s Climate Pledge commits to net-zero carbon emissions across global operations by 2040 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_10].

### GRI 306 Waste  
In 2025, Amazon diverted 84% of its operational waste from landfill and incineration without energy recovery—including 82% through recycling or composting and 2% through incineration with energy recovery [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_47]. Waste per shipped unit decreased by 5% compared to 2024 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_47][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_41]. Cardboard and wood constituted 64% of Amazon’s operational waste footprint, with nearly 100% recycled [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_47]. Amazon reused or donated 343,000 assets and materials in 2025 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_45], avoided over one million hard drive purchases since 2023 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_41], and prevented 225,000 metric tons of CO₂e through reuse and resale of data center hardware since 2020 [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_45].

### GRI 403 Occupational Health & Safety  
Amazon’s global operations safety performance demonstrated year-over-year improvement in 2025, with a 14% reduction in the Global Recordable Incident Rate (RIR) — defined as work-related injuries requiring more than basic first-aid treatment — and a 14% reduction in the Global Lost Time Incident Rate (LTIR) — defined as injuries resulting in time away from work [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_76][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_78]. Over six years, the global RIR improved by 43% and the global LTIR by 70% [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_76][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_78]. In the U.S., under the General Warehousing and Storage classification, the RIR improved 16% year over year and 39% over six years; the LTIR was flat year over year but improved 79% over six years [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_76]. Under Courier and Express Delivery Services, the RIR improved 19% year over year and 59% over six years, while the LTIR improved 28% year over year and 81% over six years [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_76]. Amazon conducted 10.4 million safety inspections globally in 2025 — a 33% increase from 2024 — to validate engineering controls and inform iterative improvements [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_77]. The company employs over 11,000 safety professionals worldwide [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_76]. *However, the report does not disclose the percentage of employees covered by occupational health services, frequency of mandatory health assessments, or baseline-aligned science-based safety targets (e.g., RIR target for 2030), as required by GRI 403-3 and TCFD-aligned goal-setting expectations.*

### Board Oversight and Committee Structure  
Amazon’s Board of Directors is responsible for the control and direction of the company and represents shareholders with the primary purpose of building long-term shareholder value [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_101]. In 2025, the Board held six in-person meetings and participated in regularly scheduled teleconference discussions [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_101]. Risk oversight is delegated to four standing committees: the Audit Committee (six meetings), Leadership Development and Compensation Committee (five meetings), Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee (four meetings), and Security Committee (two meetings) [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_102]. The Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee oversees risks related to sustainability, environmental and corporate social responsibility practices, operations and supply chain, and responsible AI development and AI governance [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_102][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_103][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_104]. ### Anti-Corruption Governance and Controls  
Amazon’s Business Conduct and Ethics program is led by the Vice President for Business Conduct and Ethics, who reports to the Senior Vice President, Chief Global Affairs & Legal Officer [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_106]. The program includes a review process for allegations—investigations are reported to the Board’s Audit Committee—and an ethics line available in 65 countries and 165 languages [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_106]. Employees are prohibited from bribing anyone, whether in dealings with governments or the private sector [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_107]. Third-party due diligence is conducted on a risk-based approach before entering business relationships, and employees must disclose and seek approval for qualifying gifts or services received from third parties or given to government officials [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_107]. Amazon participates in the OECD’s Anti-Corruption Leaders Hub (ACLH), a public-private working group focused on digitizing compliance information and expanding local partner vetting programs [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_107].

### Climate-Related and Human Rights Governance  
Climate-related governance is overseen by the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee, which receives regular updates from the Chief Sustainability Officer [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_103][2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_104]. The Sustainability team maintains a companywide carbon system of record to track emissions at the business level and reports quarterly to senior leadership on environmental and sustainability performance [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_104]. Human rights governance includes oversight by the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee for policies on human rights and ethical business practices, and by the Leadership Development and Compensation Committee for internal human capital management—including nondiscrimination, workplace safety, and culture [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_104]. Amazon employs a Human Rights Officer (HRO) who reports directly to the Chief Sustainability Officer and facilitates regular risk management updates to senior leadership [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_104].

### Shareholder Engagement and Ethics Training  
Amazon conducts year-round engagement with shareholders through a dedicated team whose full-time responsibilities include communicating with investors, management, and Board members [2025-amazon-sustainability-report_chunk_104].
