### GRI 302 Energy  
Apple achieved 100% renewable electricity for its global corporate facilities—including offices, retail stores, and data centers—starting in January 2018 [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_126]. In fiscal year 2024, Apple reported 3,777,000 MWh of renewable electricity used across corporate facilities, representing 100% of total energy use [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_125]. This includes on-site generation from a diversified portfolio of renewable technologies: solar (72%), wind (27%), microhydro (0.2%), and biogas fuel cells (0.2%), with a total operational capacity of 1,782 MW across 22 countries and regions [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_165]. For colocation facilities—third-party data centers where Apple leases capacity—100% of Apple’s energy use has been matched with renewable energy generated within the same country or regional grid since January 2018 [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_173]. In FY 2020, Apple’s colocation facilities consumed 372,901,398 kWh of electricity, all matched to renewables, resulting in zero scope 2 emissions from that segment [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_173].

### GRI 303 Water  
In fiscal year 2024, Apple’s corporate facilities withdrew 1,756 million gallons of water globally, of which 1,532 million gallons were freshwater and 197 million gallons were recycled water [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_126]. Recycled water is sourced primarily from municipal treatment plants (≥95%) and used for irrigation, cooling tower makeup, and toilet flushing [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_128]. Apple also reported 27 million gallons from “other alternative sources”, including rainwater and recovered condensate [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_127]. Total water discharge was 900 million gallons worldwide [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_181]. Apple defines facility water use as “high stress” if located in basins classified as high or extremely high baseline water stress per WRI’s Aqueduct Water Risk Atlas V4.0 [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_213]. The company certified all Apple-owned data centers to the Alliance for Water Stewardship (AWS) Standard by 2025 [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_4], and engaged in collaborative initiatives—including co-developing the Volumetric Water Benefit Accounting methodology with WRI—to advance technical best practices in water stewardship [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_74].

### GRI 305 Emissions  
Apple achieved carbon neutrality for its corporate (Scope 1 + Scope 2) emissions beginning in April 2020 and has maintained it annually through high-quality carbon credit retirements [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_118]. In fiscal year 2024, Apple’s corporate Scope 1 emissions totaled 55,200 metric tons CO₂e, and Scope 2 (market-based) emissions totaled 278,108 metric tons CO₂e across all corporate locations—including data centers, retail stores, and offices [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_166]. Notably, Apple’s Scope 2 emissions from purchased electricity are zero; however, emissions from purchased steam, heating, and cooling amounted to 3,300 metric tons CO₂e in FY 2024 [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_165]. Apple’s comprehensive carbon footprint—including Scope 3 categories such as product use (4.68 million metric tonnes), manufacturing (29.03 million metric tonnes), transportation, and recycling—was verified by a non-Apex third-party provider [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_183]. Apple’s net corporate emissions in FY 2024 were 14.5 million metric tons CO₂e (gross: 15.3 million; offsets/removal: 0.7 million) [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_9].

### GRI 306 Waste  
Apple’s corporate facilities achieved a landfill diversion rate of 70% in fiscal year 2024 [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_127]. During that year, 18,800 metric tonnes of trash were disposed of in landfills, 1,200 metric tonnes of hazardous (regulated) waste were generated, and 36,500 metric tonnes were recycled [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_181]. Additionally, 4,100 metric tonnes were composted and 1,000 metric tonnes were sent to waste-to-energy facilities [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_181]. Construction and demolition (C&D) waste totaled 5,200 metric tonnes landfilled and 22 metric tonnes recycled [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_181]. Apple’s Zero Waste Program targets elimination of landfill-bound waste from corporate facilities and suppliers [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_4].

### GRI 403 Occupational Health & Safety  
Apple’s Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) Policy states its commitment to protecting the health and safety of employees, customers, and contractors across all global operations, including design, research, manufacturing, distribution, and product use [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_201]. The policy mandates that all employees receive EHS training and tools in their primary language to ensure awareness of their roles and responsibilities in sustaining Apple’s EHS management systems [Apple_Environmental_Progress_Report_2025_chunk_201].
